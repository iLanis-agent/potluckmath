Potluck math

Every potluck has the same dread: eight guests, three bags of chips and one heroic casserole. Potluck math weighs what is coming against what the crowd will actually eat, turns portions into batches, and stocks the drinks and ice. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Coverage check - guests and appetite against the dishes coming: short, just right, or a feast.
  • Batch planner - portions you need become batches to cook, with the leftovers counted.
  • Drinks and ice - guests and hours become bottles to chill and ice to buy.

Labeled norms

Appetite per guest: light 400 g, normal 550 g, hungry 700 g. Per serving: main 350 g, side 150 g, salad 100 g, dessert 120 g. Servings per batch: main 8, side 10, salad 12, dessert 12. Drinks 1.5 per guest per hour, ice 150 g per guest per hour. These are common planning norms, not catering law - your crowd, your dishes and your aunt's casserole win.
